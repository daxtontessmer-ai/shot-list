# ChatGPT Video Ideas Research

Research notes for ideas placed only under `CHATGPT'S VIDEO IDEAS`. These notes summarize format principles; they do not copy other creators' scripts.

## 2026-10-11 — Initial format research

- YouTube says Shorts are not boosted because of a specific format. Distribution responds to whether viewers choose to watch, how long they stay, average percentage viewed, and satisfaction signals. That favors an immediately understandable first frame, a fast payoff, and a reason to rewatch. Source: [YouTube Shorts search and discovery tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225)
- YouTube recommends studying retention spikes, dips, and top moments, then moving compelling moments earlier and developing successful moments into new videos. Source: [YouTube audience-retention guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415)
- YouTube specifically highlights relatable comedy skits that use familiar audio or lyrics as a punch line, while still recommending creators focus on what their own audience enjoys. Source: [YouTube Shorts search and discovery tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225)
- YouTube advises comparing like-for-like Shorts, tracking chose-to-view, watch time, retention, likes, comments, and remixes, then repeating patterns that consistently outperform. Source: [YouTube Shorts analytics tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217)
- TikTok's official creative guidance organizes short videos around a hook, body, and close, with the opening seconds carrying disproportionate importance. Source: [TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Creative_Codes_ENG.pdf)
- TikTok Creative Center provides current trend, keyword, Top Ad, and high-engagement-moment research. Trends should inform presentation, not replace an original premise or script. Source: [TikTok Creative Center overview](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-creative-tips-finder)

## Formats to explore

1. **Impossible location match cut:** repeat the same body motion, framing, wardrobe, and sound across Fayetteville and an outdoor destination.
2. **Outdoor stereotype escalation:** establish a recognizable type immediately, heighten it through three increasingly absurd behaviors, then reverse the stereotype.
3. **Deadpan field tutorial:** treat a simple outdoor behavior with unnecessary documentary seriousness and a visual punch line.
4. **Expectation versus trail reality:** promise a cinematic adventure, then interrupt it with an honest inconvenience before earning a beautiful payoff.
5. **Repeatable friend archetypes:** use recurring characters such as the over-packer, ultralight purist, content creator, navigator, and friend who said the hike was easy.
6. **Impact loops:** end with the same movement or composition that opened the video so replay feels seamless.
